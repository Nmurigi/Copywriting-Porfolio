Menoa — sales page

The service. Menoa connects women in perimenopause and menopause with licensed clinicians online. Care can include virtual visits, support between appointments, lab tests when needed, and prescription treatment when a clinician finds it right for you. Menoa doesn't sell supplements or promise a cure.

The reader. A woman between 42 and 55 who has already spoken to her regular doctor about this at least once, and came away without much of a plan. She's been awake since 3am, and she's doing this research on her phone.

The problem this page solves. Menopause marketing tends to fail in one of two directions. Supplement brands over-promise until the reader stops believing anything that sounds excited, while clinical services under-sell until she can't work out what she'd actually be paying for. This page has to sound like a clinician who takes her seriously, and it still has to sell a subscription.

What the copy does. It opens on recognition rather than a symptom list, because she already knows her symptoms and doesn't need them read back to her. It says plainly what's included and what it costs, since anything vague reads as a trap in health services. It names the limits of what Menoa can do, which earns more trust than a testimonial would, and it keeps every claim to what the service provides rather than what it will fix.

You've already been told you're fine

Maybe your tests came back normal. Maybe you were told this is part of getting older. Maybe you left with a prescription for something you didn't go in to ask about.

But you're still waking at 3am. You're still keeping a spare shirt at work. And you're still trying to work out what changed.

So you manage it on your own, sleeping with a fan on and avoiding anything important on the days you know will be hard. That works for a while, until the day it doesn't.

This is where a menopause clinician starts

There's a lot going on at once during this transition, and it rarely arrives in a tidy order. Hot flashes and night sweats, waking at 3am and staying awake, cycles that change length or disappear and come back, joint aches that showed up without an injury, brain fog in meetings you used to run without notes, and anxiety with nothing attached to it.

Some symptoms may be linked to menopause, while others can have another cause. That's why your clinician starts with your health history instead of assuming menopause explains everything.

Menoa isn't right for everyone

Some health needs require in-person care or a different kind of specialist. Your intake helps us check that before you book, and your clinician will tell you if another type of care would suit you better.

We also don't sell supplements and we won't recommend one. If your clinician thinks a supplement could help, they'll name it and you can buy it anywhere you like.

You may have questions about hormone therapy

Your clinician will talk through the options that may suit your health history, along with the risks and benefits that apply to you. Hormone therapy isn't right for everyone, and you won't be pushed toward it.

The aim is to give you enough information to make the choice with your clinician.

You shouldn't have to take a subscription on faith

If Menoa isn't useful to you, cancel from your account and you won't be charged again. Your records download in full, so you can take them to any doctor you choose.

Your first visit is the real test, and it runs 45 minutes because that's how long the conversation takes. You'll know by the end of it what kind of care you've bought.

Questions women ask before they book

Do you take insurance?

The $45 monthly membership is paid directly and isn't billed to insurance, though prescriptions and lab work usually can be. We provide itemized receipts if you have an FSA or HSA account.

Can I keep my own doctor?

Yes, and we'd encourage it. With your permission we'll send your visit notes and any lab results to your primary care doctor.

What if I don't need a prescription?

Plenty of patients don't. Your clinician will talk through other options with you if that's the better route for your situation.

Is this available in my state?

Menoa is licensed in all 50 states, and your intake confirms your location before you book.

How long until something changes?

That depends on what you're treating and how your body responds, so your clinician will give you a realistic timeline at your first visit rather than a promise. Most plans need at least one adjustment before they settle.
