Briefly — pricing page

The product. Briefly turns meetings, calls, and client chats into assigned tasks, decisions, and follow-ups.

The buyer. Account leads at agencies running 15 to 60 client relationships at once. They sit in a lot of calls and they carry the blame when something agreed on a Tuesday call never reaches anyone's queue.

The problem this page solves. AI notetakers have trained buyers to expect three traps: minute caps that run out mid-month, per-seat pricing that punishes you for inviting a colleague to one call, and recording consent problems on client-facing meetings. A pricing page in this category that doesn't answer all three loses the reader before the plan cards.

What the copy does. Removes the meter anxiety in the first line, prices on people instead of usage, defines "user" before anyone has to guess, and handles the client-consent objection in the open rather than burying it in a security page nobody clicks.

Your clients will know they're being recorded

Briefly announces itself when it joins and asks your client to agree before it records anything. If someone declines, Briefly doesn't record the call.

Every answer is time-stamped and saved with the meeting, so you have a record if a client ever asks. On the Agency plan you can export the trail for a single client or for the whole account.

Moving off your current notetaker takes an afternoon

Import your existing transcripts and recaps from Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, or Grain, and Briefly will index them alongside the meetings it records itself. Your searchable history doesn't restart on day one.

If you're mid-contract elsewhere, we'll credit the remaining months of that subscription against your first Briefly invoice, up to six months. Send us the renewal notice and we'll apply it.

Nobody has to change how they work

Briefly joins the calendar invite you already created and posts the recap where your team already reads things. There's no new tab to check after every call or another daily habit to build.

Your team reviews each task before it reaches the project board, so people stay in control of what gets assigned.

Questions that come up before people buy

What happens when I add someone mid-month?

You pay for the days left in the month, not the full month. Remove someone and the credit lands on your next invoice without you asking.

Can I mix plans across my company?

Yes. Client services can sit on Agency while your internal marketing team sits on Team, under one bill and one admin console.

Do you train models on our meetings?

No. Your recordings, transcripts, and tasks are never used to train any model, ours or a third party's. This holds on every plan, including the free trial.

How long do you keep recordings?

Starter keeps 90 days. Team and Agency keep everything until you delete it. Enterprise sets its own retention rules, including automatic deletion at 30, 60, or 180 days.

What if the transcript gets something wrong?

Every task and decision links back to the moment in the recording it came from. Click the timestamp to hear the sentence, then fix it in place.

Can I charge Briefly back to my clients?

Yes. Tag each workspace to a client and export a monthly cost breakdown by account, ready to drop into your invoice or your retainer report.

Can I cancel?

At any time, from inside your account. On an annual plan, we refund the months you haven't used.
